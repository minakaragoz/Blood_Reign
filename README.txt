READ ME:

For this game, we made all art assets for the game. The only external assets used were the music and sound effects present in the game. These assets were public domain found by our team member Jessica. 

For this game, we spent a semester long contributing to the project. Blood Reign puts the player in a fast-paced vampire hack and slash using your vampiric roots to take back the night from a race of hunters. Your goal is to revive the dwindling vampire race. The expected playtime is about 10 minutes start to finish featuring projectiles, bat form, a working skill tree, and universal resource control for the player. Controls are outlined in the start screen.
